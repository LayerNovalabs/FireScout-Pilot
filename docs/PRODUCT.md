# FireScout Product

## Product Overview

FireScout is an Emergency Intelligence Platform designed to help emergency response organizations coordinate devices, operational data, missions, and AI-assisted decision support from a single command environment.

The platform is designed to work with both simulated and real systems through the same core architecture.

## Primary Users

FireScout is intended for organizations such as:

- Fire Departments
- Civil Protection
- Search and Rescue Teams
- Coast Guard
- Disaster Response Agencies
- Emergency Coordination Centers

## Core Product Capabilities

FireScout is designed to provide:

- Real-time device monitoring
- Multi-device mission coordination
- Live operational mapping
- Mission-specific dashboards
- Operational event detection
- AI-assisted decision support
- Mission history and replay
- Mission reporting
- Hardware-independent device integration

## Initial Competition Version

The first operational version of FireScout will focus on a self-guided simulation experience that judges can use without assistance.

The initial version will include:

- Simulated emergency missions
- Simulated autonomous devices
- Interactive mission selection
- Real-time telemetry
- Operational alerts
- Dynamic map visualization
- AI-style decision recommendations
- Mission summary and report generation

## Initial Mission Types

The first supported mission scenarios will be:

### Wildfire

Possible simulated events:

- Fire detection
- Smoke detection
- Victim detection
- High temperature zones
- Hazardous gas alerts
- Device battery warnings

### Flood

Possible simulated events:

- Flooded roads
- Isolated people
- Stranded vehicles
- Unsafe areas
- Access route recommendations

### Maritime Search and Rescue

Possible simulated events:

- Person in water
- Life raft detection
- Vessel detection
- Search area coverage
- Drift estimation

## Product Evolution

After the competition version, the same platform architecture should support real-world integrations such as:

- DJI drones
- MAVLink-compatible vehicles
- Pixhawk-based systems
- Ground robots
- Unmanned surface vehicles
- Fixed cameras
- IoT sensors
- Weather stations

The simulator should remain part of the product as a training, testing, development, and demonstration environment.

## Product Principle

The competition version is not a disposable prototype.

It is the first operational version of the FireScout product architecture.
